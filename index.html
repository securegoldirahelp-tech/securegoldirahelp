Why Consider Physical Gold

Why Retirement Investors Consider Physical Gold

Many retirees want protection from inflation, stock market volatility, bank failures, and economic uncertainty. Physical gold has historically been used as a hedge during uncertain times — inside a tax-advantaged IRA.

📈

Inflation Protection

Gold has historically maintained purchasing power over decades while paper currencies lose value to inflation.

🛡️

Market Volatility Defense

When equity markets decline, gold has often moved independently — providing a stabilizing offset in diversified portfolios.

💲

Dollar Devaluation Defense

As the U.S. dollar loses purchasing power, gold’s value — priced globally — has historically risen to compensate.

⚖️

Portfolio Diversification

Adding a physical asset that moves independently of stocks and bonds can reduce overall portfolio concentration risk.

Simple 4-Step Process

How a Gold IRA Rollover Works

Moving retirement funds into a Gold IRA is simpler than most people expect when done through a proper direct rollover. Here’s the general process:

1

Choose a Self-Directed IRA Custodian

Select an IRS-approved custodian that specializes in precious metals IRAs. We can help you understand what to look for.

2

Initiate a Direct Rollover

Request a direct rollover from your 401(k), IRA, TSP, or other qualified account. Funds move directly — you do not take personal possession.

3

Select IRS-Approved Physical Gold

Choose from IRS-approved gold coins and bars (minimum .995 purity) with guidance from your custodian’s specialist.

4

Secure Storage + Long-Term Protection

Your metals are held at an IRS-approved, insured depository vault on your behalf — you cannot store IRA metals at home.
